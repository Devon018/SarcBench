# SarcBench Research Use Terms

SarcBench is made available for peer review and non-commercial academic research
on sarcasm understanding, response generation, controlled sarcasm generation,
and benchmark evaluation.

You may use, copy, and redistribute the dataset for non-commercial research and
review purposes, provided that you preserve this notice and cite the associated
paper or repository release when appropriate.

You may not use the dataset to identify, re-identify, contact, profile, harass,
or make decisions about individuals or communities. You may not attempt to
recover platform, video, comment, or account identifiers that were removed from
the released sample IDs. You may not use the dataset for surveillance,
commercial user profiling, targeted advertising, high-stakes decision-making,
or other harmful applications.

The dataset contains excerpts of public online discussion text and benchmark
annotations. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, platform
terms, institutional review requirements, and ethical review obligations in
their jurisdiction.

The dataset is provided as-is, without warranties or guarantees of fitness for
any particular purpose.
