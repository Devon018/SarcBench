----------------------------------------
Response Style Definitions
----------------------------------------

- teasing:
  Respond playfully. The reply may extend the joke, add a mild ironic flourish,
  or lightly joke with the speaker or target. It should remain friendly and
  should not become hostile.

- pushback:
  Gently challenge or soften the sarcastic stance. The reply should offer a
  less cynical interpretation, point out that the sarcasm may be too harsh, or
  defend the target mildly while remaining conversational.

- neutral:
  Give a balanced, non-confrontational continuation. The reply should acknowledge
  the sarcastic point without strongly agreeing, teasing, or pushing back.

The paper experiment uses exactly these three requested styles: teasing,
pushback, and neutral.

----------------------------------------
Quality Requirements
----------------------------------------

The reply should:
- address the LAST comment, not an earlier comment only;
- recognize the sarcastic implication instead of taking the wording literally;
- fit the thread's language and platform style when possible;
- avoid generic filler such as "I agree" without context;
- avoid explaining what sarcasm is;
- avoid meta-commentary about being an AI model or about the benchmark;
- be concise enough to resemble an online reply, unless the context naturally
