You are participating in an online discussion.

The comment thread below stops immediately before the original sarcastic final
comment. You are given the intended meaning that the new comment should convey
and the preferred sarcasm mechanisms. Write one sarcastic comment that naturally
fits after the thread.

----------------------------------------
Goal
----------------------------------------

Generate a sarcastic comment that:
- preserves the intended meaning;
- targets the same implied object of criticism when recoverable from context;
- uses the preferred mechanisms when possible;
- fits the thread's language, tone, and platform style;
- sounds like a plausible human online comment.

Do not state the intended meaning literally as an explanation. Realize it
through a sarcastic utterance.

----------------------------------------
Mechanism Definitions
----------------------------------------

- polarity_reversal:
  Use surface praise, acceptance, calmness, or approval to imply the opposite
  critical stance.

- exaggeration:
  Use deliberate overstatement of quality, consequence, scale, certainty,
  achievement, or failure to make the criticism or ridicule clear.

- understatement:
  Deliberately downplay something more serious, absurd, awkward, bad, or
  impressive than the literal wording suggests.

- rhetorical_question:
  Use a question that is not a genuine request for information, but instead
  pushes the reader toward the intended mocking inference.

- contrast_with_context:
  Make the sarcastic force depend on the mismatch between the comment and the
  preceding thread, video context, expectations, or known situation.

- sarcasm_marker:
  Use explicit online sarcasm cues such as emoji, platform emotes, scare quotes,
  particles, stylized punctuation, or formulaic cue phrases when they fit the
  context.

- other:
  Use another sarcastic realization when the preferred mechanism cannot be
  naturally expressed with the available context.

Mechanism control guidance:
- Prefer naturalness over mechanically forcing every label.
- If multiple preferred mechanisms are listed, combine them only if the comment
  remains fluent and plausible.
- Do not mention mechanism names in the output.
